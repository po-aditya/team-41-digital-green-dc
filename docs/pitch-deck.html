01Problem Statement

FarmerChat advises 2.2 million farmers.
No one can tell, at that scale, whether the advice worked.

2.2M

farmers ask FarmerChat by text, voice or photo, and every query ends in a resolution

Small samples

Field surveys and RCTs are costly and slow, and results don't extrapolate

One size fits none

Broadcast digital surveys ask every farmer the same questions

The challenge: read the insights FarmerChat already extracts, and send each farmer a short, tap-to-answer follow-up about their own advice.
  • Ask at the right moment in the crop calendar, after the farmer could act
  • Don't bombard farmers or collect personal data, and never give new advice
  • Prove the quality against Digital Green's golden dataset before a ~10,000-farmer pilot

05Why ours · The impact

    
Quality before volume, with the evidence to back it.

    

      

        
🎯

          
It only asks when it should

          
Rules R0–R8 decide before anything is written. A missed survey is better than an irrelevant one, so precision comes first.

          
0 false positives · 55/55 reason codes

        
🛡️

          
Safe by construction

          
Questions come from a closed, vetted catalogue. No model free-text ever reaches a farmer, and content is re-checked before every send.

          
0 unsafe sends · no personal-data asks

        
⚡

          
Fast, and cheap by default

          
About 1.2 ms per survey, and a full season of 2,628 surveys in 8.3 s for $0. Bedrock is optional, behind a durable spend ledger with a hard stop.
